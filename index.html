Questions

Before you sign up

Do I need any other software?

No. This is the software. It's made for tradies who use nothing yet, just a phone and a notebook. If you already use a job app you're happy with, keep it.

Is there a contract?

No. Every package runs month to month. Cancel any time with 2 weeks' notice by email.

Will it message my customers without asking me?

You approve every invoice before it goes live. Before we chase an invoice, we text you to check it hasn't been paid. You can pause everything with one button.

What happens to my information?

It's yours. We only use it to run the service, we never sell it, and other clients can't see it. Only the companies we need to run it (hosting, texts, email and payments) handle it. Read the terms and privacy policy.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works on any phone, tablet or laptop in your browser. Nothing to download.

What do the extra texts cost?

Elevate includes about 100 texts a month and Premium about 300. Basic doesn't send any. After that it's 25 cents a text, added to your next bill. We warn you before you get there.
